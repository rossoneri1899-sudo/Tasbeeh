Hands-free Tasbeeh

Recite and it counts, with your phone face up beside you.

  1. Tap the microphone and allow access. Everything stays on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
  2. Teach it each dhikr once by saying it 5 times. After that it only counts sounds like yours, so other voices and noise don’t count.
  3. Recite at your normal pace. Each recitation becomes a bead.
  4. Tap the beads if it misses one, or Undo if it counts extra. You can also just tap to count.
