# Hands-free Tasbeeh — project notes

A web app that counts tasbeeh (Islamic phrases of remembrance) by listening to the user recite, instead of tapping. Hosted on GitHub Pages and used on an iPhone in Safari (added to the home screen). The owner also shares it with friends (an Android user reached it from a WhatsApp link and voice never worked), so it must work for people who open a shared link on any phone.

## Rebuild in progress: `beta/` (read this first)

The app is being rebuilt in `beta/`, published at `…/Tasbeeh/beta/`, while the old app in the root stays live. When the owner is happy, the beta replaces the root files. Everything below this section describes the **old** root app.

- **Files:** `beta/index.html` (markup + CSS), `beta/app.js` (all logic), `beta/adhkar.js` (generated data, don't edit by hand), `beta/sw.js`, `beta/manifest.webmanifest`. Icons and trimmed recordings are reused from the root (`ROOT` = `../` inside `beta/`). The single-file rule is dropped for the rebuild: the data alone is 150 KB.
- **Voice engine (`beta/voice.js`, b3).** No speech recognition and no model download. The AudioWorklet (inline, Blob URL; ScriptProcessor fallback) only forwards raw audio after a 100–3800 Hz band-pass; `Voice.Resampler` → 16 kHz → `Voice.Front` makes a frame every 10 ms: 12 MFCCs (c0 dropped; each frame's mel spectrum floored at 30 dB below its peak so faint background noise doesn't change the shape), loudness in dB, and voicing (peak normalised autocorrelation, 70–400 Hz, on the window decimated to 8 kHz). Front keeps 12 s of audio for clipping recordings.
  - **Teaching is the main path.** "Teach it <dhikr>": 2 s of quiet, then 5 recitations, cut by `Voice.Segmenter` and stored in IndexedDB `tasbeeh2`/`samples` as 16 kHz Int16 audio `{k: dhikr key, neg, a}`. Tapping the mic on an untaught dhikr with target ≥ 3 offers teaching first (once; "Count without teaching" sets `V.skip[k]`). Recordings from the old app's "Train it on my voice" (IndexedDB `tasbeeh`, keyed by name) are imported once by matching names.
  - **Taught (≥3 recordings): `Voice.Matcher`** = the old app's open-start subsequence DTW (SPRING) on templates trimmed to their voiced part (`Voice.template`), so back-to-back recitation works. Limit = 1.8 × the 90th percentile of leave-one-out scores between the user's own recordings, moved to the geometric mean with the closest wrong sound when there is one (other taught adhkar, except phrases contained in each other, and "not this" sounds). Synthetic tests put the user's own voice at ≤2.5× in noise and other phrases/speech at ≥3.5×. Sensitivity multiplies the limit 0.85–1.25×; the loudness gate is relaxed by 5 dB when matching.
  - **Learning by itself (b4, owner found teaching every dhikr tedious):** in rhythm mode each counted recitation of a dhikr (≤160 Arabic letters) is kept in `pend[k]`; Undo drops the last one. At `LEARN_N` = 5, `Voice.consistent()` keeps the ones whose median DTW distance to the others is ≤1.4× the typical (drops a cough or noise picked up by mistake). If ≥3 remain they become the dhikr's recordings and matching takes over. The mic no longer forces the teach sheet; "Teach it now" stays as a link.
  - **Voice profile (b4):** built from every recording of the user (any dhikr, plus optional "Train my voice", key `_voice`, 8 recordings of any adhkar) once there are 6. Rhythm-mode stretches are rejected if their median pitch is outside the user's range (5th–95th percentile × 0.75–1.3). Average MFCCs turned out to depend on the words more than the speaker (own voice saying an unheard dhikr scored further than another speaker), so they're only a loose check (2.5× the limit). So it ignores clearly higher or lower voices (other gender, children, many TV voices), not a similar voice.
  - **Corrections:** Undo after a voice count saves that sound as "not this" (automatic). Tapping the beads within 15 s of an uncounted sound offers "Save it" as a recording.
  - **Not taught: `Voice.Segmenter` only.** Speech = loud enough above the adaptive background (fast down, slow up) and pitched within the last 120 ms, so hiss and clatter don't count. A stretch ends after `gap` ms (default 350) without speech; over 12 s it's rejected (talking or a steady sound, which becomes the background). Counts a stretch 280 ms–6 s long, or 0.45–2.2× the learned length once it has seen 3. Splitting long stretches is off by default (b1 split one slow SubhanAllah into 3–4 counts: its guessed length from letter count was 0.56 s; a deliberate recitation is 1.4–1.9 s). This mode can't tell words apart, and the hint under the beads says so.
  - **Targets (b4):** Target button under the beads → presets, own number, or No limit (stored as `NOLIMIT` = -1, `target()` returns Infinity, beads loop in 33s), applied to this dhikr / this list / everything. Stored per entry in `st.n` (only when different from the usual).
  - **Tests:** `tools/voice-test.html` (open via the local server) runs 21 synthetic-audio cases (b4 added the profile and the learning filter); originally 16: pauses, slow, varied speed, back-to-back, quiet, hiss, similar vowels, one syllable changed, another dhikr, TV-like speech, noise bursts, clatter. All pass. Analysis + matching takes 0.4% of real time on a desktop. Real voices vary more than synthetic ones; tune the 1.8 factor from owner reports (the status line shows "match x of limit").
- **Shared-link problems are handled up front:** `inAppBrowser()` detects WhatsApp/Instagram/Facebook/TikTok/etc. and Android WebViews (`; wv)`) and iOS WKWebViews (no `Safari/` in the UA), and shows a banner with an "Open in Chrome" `intent://` link on Android or Safari steps on iPhone, plus Copy link. Mic errors map to short messages with a "How to fix" button that opens **Check my setup** (`diagnostics()`): a verdict, a 6 s live mic test, and copyable details for the owner to diagnose friends' phones. The Share button sends instructions to open in Chrome/Safari.
- **Content:** 215 dhikrs in 20 categories, all from Hisn al-Muslim plus 33 Quranic duas and a few short phrases. Built by `tools/build_adhkar.py` from rn0x/Adhkar-json (Arabic + recordings), the Hugging Face dataset M-AI-C/hisnulmuslim (transliteration, English, references; matched to the Arabic by text, since the numbering is off by one in places) and api.alquran.cloud (Uthmani text, Sahih International). The script holds all curation: category lists, cuts of narrative text (`start`/`end` markers on diacritic-free text), evening wordings written out from the book's notes, and plain-English titles (`NAMES`). Download the sources to a folder and run `python tools/build_adhkar.py <sources dir> beta/adhkar.js review.txt`, then read review.txt.
- **Recordings:** the 16 trimmed files in `audio/` are used where they exist; 144 more stream on demand from jsDelivr (`AUDIO_CDN`, pinned to an rn0x commit). Those are untrimmed, so some start with the spoken chapter title. Quran duas play the full verse from everyayah.com.
- **Storage:** `localStorage` keys prefixed `tb2.` so the old app on the same origin is unaffected. On first run the old `history` is imported so streaks continue. Counts reset at local midnight; history keeps 800 days.
- **Versioning:** bump `APP_VERSION` in `beta/app.js` (the update check reads `app.js`), and `CACHE` in `beta/sw.js` when caching rules change.
- After-prayer tasbeeh is 33/33/34 (the owner's choice), not Hisn al-Muslim's 33/33/33 + La ilaha illallah.

## Constraints (read first)

- **The owner uses an iPhone and has no Mac.** A native iOS app is off the table for now. Everything must work as a web page in iOS Safari.
- **Hosted on GitHub Pages** from the `main` branch root. Deploying = committing `index.html` to `main`. **Bump `APP_VERSION` in `index.html` on every deploy**: the page fetches the live copy (no-store) on launch and whenever it returns to the foreground, and shows a "new version, tap to update" banner if the number differs. This exists because iOS resumes home-screen apps from memory instead of reloading them. The version is shown at the bottom of the page, so ask the owner which number they see when an update seems missing.
- **On-device only.** No audio may leave the phone. Do not add server-side speech APIs.
- **Do not use the Web Speech API** (`SpeechRecognition` / `webkitSpeechRecognition`). It is unavailable or blocked on iPhone; this was the first approach and it failed.
- **Single self-contained file.** Keep markup, styles and script in `index.html`. External scripts only from a CDN. Exception: files browsers require to be separate — `sw.js` (service worker), `manifest.webmanifest`, and the icons `icon-180.png` / `icon-192.png` / `icon-512.png`.
- The owner tests on their phone and reports back. There is no automated test setup, so keep changes small and easy to verify by hand. After each deploy, remind them to reload with a new `?v=N` query string, because Safari caches the old page. (The service worker is network-first, so this matters less now, but it is still the safe advice.)

## How it works now

- **Offline / home screen:** `manifest.webmanifest` + `apple-touch-icon` (prayer-bead icon). `sw.js` caches the page network-first with `cache:'no-cache'` (GitHub Pages sends `max-age=600`, which otherwise served stale pages for 10 min), 4 s timeout, ignores `?v=N`, and `cdn.jsdelivr.net` cache-first; it precaches the transformers.js entry. Whisper model files are cached by transformers.js itself (`transformers-cache`), which the SW never deletes. Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` when its caching rules change. transformers.js is loaded with a dynamic `import()` inside `loadModel()`, so voice-sample counting works even if the CDN is unreachable.
- **Speech engine:** Whisper tiny (`Xenova/whisper-tiny`, q8) running in the page via transformers.js (`@huggingface/transformers@3.5.1` from jsDelivr). ~40 MB download on first use, cached by the browser after that.
- **Audio capture:** `getUserMedia` → `AudioContext` → `ScriptProcessorNode` (4096 frames). Resampled to 16 kHz before transcription.
- **Voice-activity segmenter:** RMS energy against an adaptive noise floor (`thr = max(0.012, noise*3)`). Keeps ~3 buffers of pre-roll. A segment is sent to Whisper after `PAUSE = 0.45` s of quiet or when it reaches 6 s. Segments under 0.4 s are dropped. Segments queue and are transcribed one at a time.
- **Matching:** Arabic text is normalized by `norm()` (strips diacritics/tatweel, unifies alef / ta marbuta / alef maqsura, removes spaces and non-Arabic). Built-in tasbeehs use regexes on the normalized text; matches are counted per segment.
- **Screen wake lock** is requested while listening, since iOS stops audio when the screen locks.
- **Voice-sample engine (no Whisper):** if the current tasbeeh has ≥3 recorded samples and "Count using my voice samples" is on, listening skips Whisper entirely (no model download). Audio is turned into 12 MFCCs (c0 dropped so volume/whispering don't matter) every 10 ms, and every sample is matched against the live stream with open-start subsequence DTW (SPRING). A count fires at the lowest score once it hasn't improved for 120 ms; paths that started before that end are cleared so one recitation counts once. Matches must be 0.5–2× the sample's length and ≥50% above the energy threshold. Back-to-back recitation without pauses works.
- **Threshold** is calibrated from the samples: 90th percentile of leave-one-out scores × 1.15, lowered if another tasbeeh's samples score too close (tasbeehs whose text contains the other, like SubhanAllah / ...wa bihamdihi, are not compared). The user's sensitivity slider (0.7–1.5×, `localStorage` `vsens`) multiplies it. The status line shows the match score and limit on every count, and near misses (within 1.5× of the limit), so the owner can report numbers.

## Features

- **Phrase library + categories.** `P` holds 26 phrases (Arabic with harakat, `tr` name, English meaning `en`, optional regex `re`). `CATS` lists categories with per-category targets: Everyday, After prayer (Astaghfirullah 3, Allahumma antas-salam, 33/33/34, Ayat al-Kursi), Morning and Evening (selection from Hisn al-Muslim: Ayat al-Kursi, 3 Quls ×3, Sayyid al-Istighfar, Raditu ×3, Bismillahilladhi ×3, 'Afini ×3, Hasbiyallahu ×7, 'adada khalqihi ×3 (morning) / A'udhu bi kalimatillah ×3 (evening), Tahlil wahdahu ×10, SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi ×100, Astaghfirullaha wa atubu ilayh ×100), Friday (Salawat ×100, Sallallahu alayhi wa sallam ×100, Salawat Ibrahimiyya ×10, Astaghfirullah ×100), My tasbeehs (custom; tab hidden when empty). `T` is the flat list of entries (a phrase can appear in several categories with separate counts). Learned spellings and voice samples are keyed by `tr`, so they are shared across categories.
- Matching: phrases with `re` use the regex; others (and custom ones) use `fuzzy()`. Only the selected entry is ever counted, so overlapping phrases (SubhanAllah inside SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi) don't matter. Phrases over 60 normalized letters (Ayat al-Kursi, Quls, Sayyid al-Istighfar, etc.) are tap-only; a hint says so. Long texts get a smaller font, capped at 40vh with scrolling.
- **Navigation: left panel.** A menu button (top-left) opens a slide-in panel (`#side`, `renderList()`) with collapsible category sections (header shows "N of M done"), each listing its tasbeehs with progress (`×target`, `count/target`, or ✓). Picking one closes the panel; closes also on scrim tap, ✕, Esc, or swiping left. The current category is expanded automatically. At ≥900px wide the panel stays open as a sidebar. Under the title, `#crumb` shows "Category · i of n".
- **Pronunciation button** (speaker icon left of the Arabic). Human voices only; no synthetic speech (the owner found it sounded wrong).
  - Quran passages (`q: [[surah, ayah], …]`: Ayat al-Kursi, al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, an-Nas) stream Mishary Alafasy from `https://everyayah.com/data/Alafasy_128kbps/SSSAAA.mp3`, ayah by ayah (needs a connection).
  - 16 adhkar have `au: 'audio/<key>.mp3'`: Hamad Al-Duraihim's Hisn al-Muslim recordings (hisnmuslim.com), taken from github.com/rn0x/Adhkar-json (README: free to use for anything). Each was trimmed to the dhikr only (the originals include a spoken chapter heading and/or the count note, e.g. "thalātha marrāt"), cover art stripped, 64 kbps mono, 1.4 MB total. Cuts were placed at the quietest point near Whisper word timings, and the trimmed files were re-transcribed (Whisper medium, via a temporary GitHub Actions job) to confirm each contains only its dhikr. Source map: sub 240, akb 241, tah 245, sbh 91, hsb 128, yun 124, atb 96, kal 216, hsy 83, bsm 86, rdt 87, whd 93, adk 94, sys 79, afi 82, ibr 53.
  - No recording (speaker hidden): Alhamdulillah, Astaghfirullah, La hawla, short Salawat, Sallallahu alayhi wa sallam, SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi SubhanAllahil Azim, Allahumma antas-salam, and custom tasbeehs. Hisn al-Muslim only records these inside longer passages.
  - One reused `Audio` element (iOS keeps a gesture-unlocked element playable). `sw.js` doesn't intercept `/audio/` (Safari media needs 206 range responses). While audio plays `onAudio` ignores the mic (+400 ms) and the voice detector restarts after.
  - Allahumma 'afini text is the full Hisn al-Muslim dua (including "…minal-kufri wal-faqr… 'adhabil-qabr").
- **History & streaks.** `saveProgress()` also writes today's snapshot to `localStorage` `history` = {"YYYY-MM-DD" (local date): {t: total, c: {cat|tr: count}, d: [category ids fully completed]}}, pruned to 400 days. `checkDay()` (on every count and when the app returns to the foreground) starts a fresh day after midnight. Old `progress` from a previous day is migrated into history on load. The History sheet (calendar button top-right, or the bottom of the left panel) shows: current streak (days with any dhikr; an unfinished today doesn't break it), best streak, this week, all time; routine streaks for Morning, Evening, After prayer (whole list finished); a month calendar shaded in one hue by daily total (bins 1–33, 34–99, 100–299, 300+; empty days are outlined), and a breakdown of the tapped day.
- **Auto-advance** (checkbox, on by default): on reaching a target, moves to the next unfinished entry in the same category; "All done" at the end. Finished entries get a ✓ in the list.
- **Progress persists for the day** in `localStorage` key `progress` ({day, c: counts, n: edited targets}, keyed `cat|tr`). Counts reset on a new day; edited targets are kept. Selection is kept in `sel`. "Reset whole list" clears the current category.
- Progress-ring counter that also works by tapping; undo; reset; editable target per tasbeeh.
- Vibration on each count and on reaching the target (`navigator.vibrate` does nothing on iOS).
- **Custom tasbeehs:** name, Arabic phrase, target. "Use what it last heard" fills the phrase from the last transcript. Stored in `localStorage` key `customT`. Matched with `fuzzy()`: Levenshtein against sliding windows (length L, L−1, L+1), tolerance 20% of phrase length.
- **Learn from corrections:** after each transcript a row appears; the user sets how many they actually recited and taps "Count it & learn". The normalized transcript is divided by that count and the first slice is saved as an alias for the current tasbeeh (`localStorage` key `aliases`, keyed by tasbeeh name, max 20 each). Aliases are only checked when the built-in/custom match returns 0. "Forget learned spellings" clears them.
- "Test without speaking" box: type Arabic to test matching (Whisper path only).
- **Train it on my voice:** "Record samples" uses the pause segmenter; each phrase becomes one sample (trimmed, 0.25–4 s). Samples are stored as 16 kHz Int16 audio in IndexedDB (`tasbeeh` db, `samples` store, `{t: tasbeeh name, a, at}`) so a better model can retrain from them later. Play last / Delete last / Delete all. `navigator.storage.persist()` is requested. Deleting a custom tasbeeh deletes its samples. Enrollment and voice listening use a lower energy floor (0.006) than Whisper (0.012) to catch whispers.
- **Learning from corrections (voice mode).** The last 10 s of 16 kHz audio is kept in a ring buffer (frame t = sample t·HOP). A voiced run that ends without a count becomes `lastMiss`; each count keeps its matched audio as `lastHit`. Tapping the counter while listening by voice (within 15 s of an unmatched sound, 0.25–4 s after trimming) shows a prompt "Missed one? Save … as a sample"; Undo after a voice count shows "Counted by mistake? Teach it …", which saves the audio under `name + '#not'`. Those "not this" sounds always count as negatives in `calibrate()` (which can then lower the threshold, never below median×1.05). Both are opt-in buttons that hide after 20 s, so coughs and noise aren't learned by accident. "Delete all" removes both kinds.
- Tested in headless Chromium with espeak-ng Arabic fed in as a fake mic: 5/5 SubhanAllah counted, 0 false counts on Alhamdulillah, 4/4 back-to-back. Synthetic voices are very consistent, so real-voice thresholds will be looser.

## Known issues and limits

- Noticeable delay between reciting and the count moving: the pause wait plus Whisper inference time on the phone. Pause was cut from 0.7 s to 0.45 s; watch for phrases being split and double/under counted. If so, raise toward 0.55 s.
- Whispered or very fast, run-together recitation is often missed.
- Counting stops if the screen locks or the user switches apps (iOS limitation for web pages).
- `ScriptProcessorNode` is deprecated; an `AudioWorklet` would be cleaner.
- iOS can clear `localStorage` for home-screen web apps that go unused for a while.
- Nothing has been tested by Claude on a real iPhone; all verification has come from the owner.
